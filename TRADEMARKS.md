# KINO marks

The source licenses do not grant trademark rights.

KINO, KINO D4, KINO Studio, KINO Roll, KINO Twin, the KINO wordmarks, the D4 marks, and the associated visual identity identify this project and its official releases. The logo and product-media files under `docs/assets/brand/` and `docs/assets/product/` are reserved material.

## Factual use

You may use the names when truthfully describing compatibility, origin, or the license notices. Examples include “based on the KINO D4 open hardware design” and “compatible with KINO Device Protocol 1.”

Factual use must not imply that a modified build, product, service, or company is maintained, approved, manufactured, or warranted by the KINO project.

## Permission required

Ask before using a KINO name or mark:

- as the name of a company, product, service, package, domain, or event;
- on the exterior, packaging, advertising, or sales page of a modified physical product;
- in a logo, icon, social-media identity, or merchandise;
- in any way that suggests an official release or endorsement.

## Forks and modified products

Public forks may retain factual copyright and license notices. A distributed modified application should use a distinct product name and replace the KINO logo unless written permission says otherwise.

A physical product made from CERN-OHL-S-2.0 source may state its design origin and source location. It must not be sold as an official KINO D4 without permission.
