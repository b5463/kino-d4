## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not AI-generated, and not close.** Both reviewers independently reached that verdict on evidence rather than vibe: hand-placed pixel-rect icons sharing drawing primitives, a `Led` component whose required `label` prop makes colour-only state structurally impossible, a timing module that refuses to collapse three metrics into the one flattering number, and a CSS comment explaining that `µs` must not be uppercased. Copy carries no flavour prose anywhere in scope.

**Deterministic scan** (`detect.mjs --json src`): exit 2, 4 findings, all `warning`.
- `side-tab` ×2 — `pages.css:106` (`.readybar` 4px), `ui.css:1047` (`.notice` 3px). **True positives** against the side-stripe ban. Design review found two more the detector missed: `.timing-metric--primary` 4px (`pages.css:816`) and `.recipe-item[aria-pressed]` `inset 2px 0 0` (`pages.css:297`).
- `layout-transition` ×1 — `ui.css:926` `.meter-fill { transition: width }`. True on the letter; measured 60fps with 0 long tasks, contained by `overflow:hidden` on a small bar.
- `broken-image` ×1 — `ShootPage.tsx` viewfinder `<img>`. **False positive**: `src` is assigned per frame from an object URL and the element only mounts while running; verified `complete === true`, `naturalWidth 320`.
- No gradient rule fired, so the anticipated era-aesthetic false positive did not occur.

**Where the machine beat the reviewers:** the worst contrast defect in the app was invisible to both design passes — `.microlabel` inside `.debugpanel-head` computes **1.27:1** (`--text-mut` overriding the header's white on a blue gradient), affecting "SIMULATOR FAULTS" on every page in Developer Mode.

**Clean bills of health:** zero console errors across 11 pages and every bench; no horizontal document overflow at 1024/1280/1680/2560/3840; Tahoma and Consolas both genuinely resolve (no silent fallback, no web fonts); 60fps with 0 long tasks including a running viewfinder; every focusable element has a visible indicator and every custom control is a real Tab-reachable `<button>`.

## Overall Impression

The architecture is better than the interface. The draft/apply model, capability gating, per-target firmware retry and three-metric timing are genuinely excellent engineering — and then ordinary sidebar navigation silently throws a draft away, the confirm dialog pre-arms its own destructive button, and four benches will contend on one UART and export contradictory numbers without saying so. Nothing here is a taste problem. The single biggest opportunity: make the lifecycle match the architecture that already exists.

## What's Working

1. **`Icon.tsx` — the icons are drawn, not sourced.** Every glyph is `[x,y,w,h,color]` rects on a 16×16 grid with `crispEdges` and a fixed 9-value palette, sharing `cameraBody()` / `lens()` primitives. That shared construction is why the toolbar reads as one set, and it is the hardest thing to fake in this idiom.
2. **`Led.tsx` forbids colour-only state at the type level.** The dot is `aria-hidden`, `label` is required, and `StatusBar` doubles it again with ✓/✗/… glyphs. An entire class of accessibility bug is impossible by construction.
3. **`useDraft` + restore diff.** Device telemetry explicitly refuses to clobber pending edits (`useDraft.ts:22`), and `DevicePage` renders a NOW/AFTER `diffConfigs` table before overwriting device state, then admits on failure that the camera may be partially restored. That honesty is worth more than the panel.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Unsaved drafts die on navigation, silently.** Measured: change JPEG QUALITY 86→87, click Quad, click back — 86, no amber bar, no warning. `useDraft` lives in the page component and `App.tsx:199` unmounts it; `beforeunload` is unset. On Quad this discards a hand-tuned four-slot set. The app raises a bold orange promise and breaks it on the next ordinary click. → Hoist drafts above the page swap, gate `goto()` behind a confirm while dirty, register `beforeunload`.

**[P0] The Looks preview is an upscaled thumbnail whose controls are a screen away.** `lookPreview.ts` fixes the canvas at 340×255; CSS paints it at 1141×856 at 1680 (3.36×, dpr 1.0), so 1px grain becomes 3px blobs. `.looks-editor`'s two-column rule is gated at `min-width: 1800px`, so at 1280 and 1680 the editor stacks *below* the preview (EDITOR title measured at y=1098 in a 950px viewport). The one reason a live preview exists is to watch it while dragging. → Size the backing store from `clientWidth × dpr`; move the two-column breakpoint to 1200px.

**[P1] `ConfirmDialog` pre-focuses the destructive action.** `confirmRef.current?.focus()` runs unconditionally, so FACTORY RESET opens with `ERASE EVERYTHING` focused and one Space/Enter commits it. Same component guards delete-capture, delete-look, reset-calibration and firmware flash. `aria-describedby` is null; Escape drops focus to `<body>`. → Focus cancel when `danger`, wire `aria-labelledby`/`describedby`, restore focus to the trigger.

**[P1] No device-busy lock.** Every bench owns a private `running` flag and consults none of the others. Four concurrent runs on one UART produced 17–21 KB/s (burn-in) against 78–174 KB/s (link bench) for the same link in the same window — contamination presented as measurement, exportable to JSON. → One `deviceBusy` claim in the store; all benches acquire it; surface the owner in the status bar.

**[P1] The capture inspector is a modal without a modal's contract.** `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` with none of the machinery: Escape does nothing (reproduced 3×), nothing is focused on open, Tab walks the page behind the scrim, and backdrop mousedown discards unsaved align offsets with no confirm. Its 10fps autoplay starts on open and bypasses `prefers-reduced-motion` because it is a JS interval. In one run a stuck overlay intercepted all pointer events. → Extract the focus-trap/Escape/restore effect from `ConfirmDialog` into a shared hook; guard close while align is dirty; default autoplay to paused under reduced motion.

**[P1] Reduced motion is honoured in CSS and ignored in JS.** `base.css:97` neutralises CSS animation, but the WiggleViz sequencer (`setTimeout`, 10Hz, perpetual on a settings page), the inspector wigglegram and the viewfinder poll all keep animating under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Verified with the media query matching.

**[P1] Focus indication is near-invisible, and inputs opt out.** `outline: 1px dotted var(--text)` measures 3.24:1 on a selected `.seg-opt`/`.nav-item` and fails WCAG 2.4.13's 2px floor; `ui.css:766` then sets `outline: none` on every `select.input`/`input.input:focus`, replacing it with a `#6ea3e8` glow at 2.60:1 — below the 3:1 non-text minimum.

**[P2] Measured contrast failures on the strings that matter most.** Worst-first: `.microlabel` in the Simulator Faults header **1.27:1**; `.nav-badge` and `.tag--accent` white-on-orange **2.49:1**; log timestamps `#97a3b2` **2.56:1**; white on `--grad-sel` top **2.87:1** (every selected sidebar item, every selected segment, every dialog title); `UNSAVED CHANGES` **3.01:1**; `--text-faint` **3.18:1** on panels — the colour of nearly every empty and loading state, plus "SKIP" in self-test results; `.modecard-desc` on a pressed mode card **4.41:1**. White on `.btn--primary` fails across the top ~45% of its gradient (2.26–3.65) — that is APPLY TO KINO, RUN SELF TEST and REBOOT.

**[P2] Touch/pointer targets below the 24px floor.** `input[type=range]` 5px track (18px thumb) for WIGGLE SPEED, JPEG QUALITY, EXPOSURE BIAS, VOLUME with no numeric alternative; Bring-Up checkboxes 14px ×38; `.toggle` 16px for `role="switch"`; `.logfilter` 19.3px; `.btn--sm` 20px — including every action in the capture inspector, where DELETE sits among ten export chips differentiated by red text alone.

**[P2] Two occlusions and a 1024 overflow.** The sticky `.applybar` permanently covers the bottom field of the scroll region (measured hiding BLACK POINT, then RGB MATRIX) with no `scroll-padding-bottom`; the fixed `.debugpanel` half-covers the Bring-Up inline test readout even when collapsed. At 1024 only, Calibration's table overflows its panel by 41px and a Device `.field` by 18px; inside the inspector `.sliderwrap` overflows by 78px at every width.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user)** — No Alt mnemonics, no Ctrl+S on APPLY, no F5 on SYNC, and `MenuBar` declares `role="menu"` while handling only Escape: the chrome promises keyboard driving and delivers mouse-only. 19 Tab stops before content, no skip link. `cursor: default` on 12 interactive classes makes `.camcard`/`.modecard`/`.recipe-item` indistinguishable from static panels until hover. COPY TO overwrites a tuned slot with no confirmation and no feedback — he clicks twice because nothing acknowledged the first. DISCARD is a borderless ghost 50px from the blue primary, no confirm, no undo.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)** — Page changes are announced to nobody: no focus move into `main`, no live region, `document.title` never changes. Every dropdown's focus ring is removed and replaced at 2.60:1. `.toggle` is 68×16 for a `role="switch"`; sliders are 5px tall with no numeric entry. `--text-faint` fails AA everywhere it appears and carries real status words. Disabled text sits at 2.10–2.48:1 *and* the app uses `disabled` for busy, so on first launch the entire toolbar and the 9-item View menu are functionally invisible. `MenuBar` wraps each `role="menuitem"` in a `<span>`, breaking required `menu > menuitem` containment. WiggleViz flashes at 10Hz forever under reduced motion.

**Riley (stress tester)** — Breaks it in under two minutes: four concurrent benches with contradictory exported numbers; Enter on FACTORY RESET wipes the device from pre-armed focus; EXIT MAINTENANCE is always enabled regardless of state; Escape does nothing in the inspector; backdrop click discards align work; ADOPT changes the live UART baud with no confirm and no stated rollback; keyboard-started calibration drops focus to `<body>` for 8 seconds because `busy` sets `disabled`; the baud ladder's fastest 4-frame time sits on the row marked ERRORS, unsuppressed.

**Jordan (first-timer)** — Opens Looks at 1680 to a huge soft image with no visible controls, scrolls a full page to find a slider, and the preview is now off-screen. The bar that appears says SAVE AS CUSTOM **RECIPE** on a page called Looks; DELETE RECIPE and "custom recipes" leak the retired term twice more. Reads `Look: SUPERIA` in a capture but `Superia` in the library. Sees "Trigger skew 77 µs" in metadata after Developer explained at length that trigger skew is the number that doesn't matter. Confirms ARM RECOVERY through a dire warning and is told the feature isn't implemented.

## Minor Observations

`var(--line)` undefined — the release-notes divider computes to `width: 0px` and silently doesn't render. `.btn--danger-solid`, `.panel-actions`, `.btn--lg` defined and never used, while FACTORY RESET — the most destructive action in the product — renders *quieter* than the blue UPDATE KINO above it. `textarea.input` matches no CSS rule (browser-default border, no bevel) and is unlabelled. `TARGET ≤ 1–2 MS` uppercases a unit via `.microlabel` — the exact mistake the neighbouring CSS comment exists to prevent. `formatUs` switches unit per value inside one column (`7.48 ms` above `139 µs`), defeating the scan the column exists for. LENS SPACING shows bare `0 / 19 / 38 / 57` while every slider carries its unit. `P4 temp 45 °C` vs `Camera temps 41° / 37°`. `1600x1200` vs `2048×1536`. `MiniHist` is 64×22 for 16 bins with `Math.max(1, …)`, so empty bins draw a dotted baseline that reads as an artifact, and all four share one `aria-label` with no camera in it. `Sparkline` autoscales with no zero baseline, so a 0.08V sag renders as a cliff directly under copy telling you to watch the sag trend. `PhasePanel` discards the sign its neighbouring column preserves, and CAM2 reads `0 µs` there but non-zero in TimingBench. `.datarow` label→value gap reaches 1319px at 3840. `.wiggleviz` is centred while every sibling row uses the 160px label column. `.notice--err` (red) used for a non-error consequence on Wiggle. `look(s)` renders "0 custom look(s)". Restoring the last page from localStorage can open the app onto Developer or Bring-Up. Gallery fetches thumbs two at a time with no aggregate progress. `capturecard-fav` puts `aria-label` on a roleless `<span>`.

## Questions to Consider

1. **The draft model is the best idea in the codebase and a sidebar click throws it away.** What if the draft belonged to the camera *session* rather than a React component — one dirty set spanning Shoot, Wiggle and Quad, with a single APPLY in the status bar reading "3 changes on 2 pages"? That would make the amber bar a promise the architecture can keep.
2. **Developer argues that a shared trigger edge to 100µs can still mean 30ms of exposure skew. Does the tool apply that scepticism to itself?** Four benches ran concurrently and disagreed 4–10× on the same link. If it won't state when its own measurement is contended, why trust the number it exports?
3. **This is a Win32 menu bar that cannot be driven from the keyboard.** For the one user who exists — the owner, servicing his own camera hundreds of times — is the 2003 language being honoured, or only drawn? Alt+C,R is *more* era-authentic than the mouse path.
4. **What is the capture inspector actually for?** A player, a frame strip, eleven exports, a stats table, seven metadata rows and a device-wide calibration editor, in a 760px box with no Escape and no way to reach the next of 22 captures. Is the modal serving the task, or was it the cheapest way to avoid designing a route?
