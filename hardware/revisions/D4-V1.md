## Fixed architecture

- One Guition JC4880P443C-I-W ESP32-P4 and ESP32-C6 main module.
- Four Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense camera nodes with OV3660 sensors.
- Four independent switched 5 V camera channels.
- Four full-duplex UART links and one shared sync branch per camera.
- Central microSD storage.
- One 1S LiPo power system with an F3A fuse, protection board, and SW6106 boost stage.
- One constant-current direct-flash assembly.

## Open items

- Final P4 GPIO assignments. The JP1 header map is corrected to the manufacturer pinout (ECN-0001); the camera UART and sync assignments on it are PROVISIONAL, and FLASH_EN and CAM_PWR_EN have no header pin.
- Measured main-board, battery, BMS, carrier, speaker, and connector envelopes.
- Locked enclosure CAD.
- Locked carrier layout or PCB.
- Closed-enclosure thermal record.
- Effective exposure-skew record from physical hardware.
- Full acceptance and party-duration soak.

## Compatibility rule

A V1-compatible change preserves the camera-node harness, supply topology, controller family, lens order, service openings, and firmware-visible hardware identity. Breaking any of those requires a revision review and may require D4 V2.
